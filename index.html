Featured · Data analytics

Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

Google Data Analytics capstone · Divvy trip data, January–May 2025

How do casual riders and annual members use bike-share differently? I cleaned and analyzed 1,430,076 trip records in Google BigQuery, comparing ride duration, weekday activity, monthly trends, and hourly patterns. I then built an interactive Tableau dashboard with four visualizations and wrote marketing recommendations for converting casual riders into members.
